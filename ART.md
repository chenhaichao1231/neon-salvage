# 原创舰船美术 / Original spacecraft art

六种舰船图为本项目原创矢量造型渲染的透明 PNG，包含装甲分区、座舱、发动机、炮组与不同轮廓。图片嵌入 art.js，离线运行无需请求外部网站。

The six ship sprites are original vector designs rendered to transparent PNGs, with armor panels, cockpit glass, engines, weapons and distinct silhouettes. Images are embedded in art.js and require no external requests.

已检查 512×512 RGBA 素材和整组预览；渲染分支测试不是浏览器视觉或手机验证。

The 512×512 RGBA assets and contact sheet were inspected. Render-branch tests are not browser visual or mobile verification.
